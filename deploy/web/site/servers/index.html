Server list and player counts as of 22:14 UTC, 17 October 2026. This page refreshes them live when it can.

There are 46 public Asheron's Call servers on the community list. By the TreeStats plugin's counts, the busiest right now are Coldeve (about 700 players), InfiniteLeaftide (about 320 players) and FrostfACE (about 300 players). webAC works with 44 of them in your browser; Conquest and AChard block its relay.

Which world should I pick?

  • Coldeve: the end of retail, and the most players of any world. A good first stop.
  • Levistras: the end of retail with a strict no-botting rule.
  • Seedsow: the classic Dark Majesty era, before Throne of Destiny. Snowreap is its PvP twin.
  • AC1999: the game as it launched in 1999.
  • Something new: custom worlds such as InfiniteLeaftide, PortalStorm and DreamWeave add levels, content and systems.

Each world has its own rules on accounts per person and on automation. Read them on the world's site or Discord, linked from its name below.

Questions

How many Asheron's Call servers are there?

The community server list that ThwargLauncher reads has 46 public worlds, and a few more run outside it. webAC can enter all but 2 of the listed worlds (Conquest and AChard refuse its relay).

Which is the most popular server?

By the player counts the TreeStats plugin reports, Coldeve (about 700 players). Those counts are a floor: only players running the plugin are counted.

Are there PvP servers?

Yes. The Type column marks them; PvP worlds include Snowreap, CABA, Doctide, Ebontide and FunkyTown PK.

Can I play these servers in my browser?

Yes, with webAC: pick the world in its launcher, or use the Play link beside it here. GDLE, classic and 1999 worlds are in beta.
