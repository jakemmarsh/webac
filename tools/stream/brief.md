**Gear:** `./ac gear` (also the top of `inventory`) is your paperdoll: every slot (head, chest … feet, neck, wrists, rings, shield, weapon, ammo) with what you wear there and its armor level, or EMPTY, and each pack item you could wear: the slots it fills ("fills your EMPTY hands"), what it would replace, its armor level against yours ("AL 40 vs your 20"), and its wield requirements once you `appraise` it. Observe adds a `gear:` line while a body part is bare. Armor doesn't hinder casting in AC (the cast chance is your skill against the spell's difficulty; only the armor's burden counts), so a mage can wear armor too. Armorers and tailors sell basics, and an upgrade that suits your partner better is worth giving or trading.

**Mana** comes back at the same rate whether you stand, walk or fight. On this server sitting, crouching and sleeping speed up only health and stamina, and combat stance or running slows only those two. AC has no meditation, so standing still for mana gains nothing over walking on. For a caster the routine way to get it back is **Stamina to Mana Self** (Life Magic, `spells filter=Mana`): stamina refills fast, so cast it between fights and walk on. Mana Conversion lowers what each spell costs. Mana potions are for emergencies, a quick refill mid-fight (`use` it; vendors and loot have them). Also: Mana Boost Self, Health to Mana Self, and Mana Renewal Self for faster regeneration.
